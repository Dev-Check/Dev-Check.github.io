About Me

Hello! My name is D'vin Johnson. I am a software developer who enjoy building projects that align with my creativity and interests. My main interests in this industry are database systems, artificial intellingence, and software development. I enjoy creating applications ranging from AI systems to database-backed applications inspired by the video games and technology I enjoy.

My Story

My interest in software and technology began with my father, who has worked with these technologies throughout my life. Watching him work became one of my biggest influences in pursuing a career focused on software development and database systems. Another major influence has been my passion for video games. I have had a controller in my hand for as long as I can remember, and my love for gaming eventually grew into a curiosity about the technology behind the experiences I enjoyed. Learning how my favorite games handled systems, mechanics, and data sparked my interest in understanding how software works behind the scenes. This curiosity led me to pursue my Bachelor of Science in Computer and Information Sciences at Harrisburg University of Science and Technology. During my studies, I developed experience with multiple programming languages, software development practices, database systems, and artificial intelligence. Through experiences such as my internship with the HU Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, where I helped build applications with a small development team, my contract position at Foot Locker as a Server Technician, and my current role as an IT Help Desk Technician at Lincoln Intermediate Unit 12, I have continued developing my technical skills while discovering the direction I want to take in my professional career.

What I Build

I enjoy building software that combines creativity, problem-solving, and technology to create useful experiences. My projects often begin with a simple question: "How can I improve something I already enjoy or solve a problem I encounter?" My work focuses on three main areas: artificial intelligence, database systems, and software development. I enjoy exploring AI through projects such as computer vision applications and machine learning models, while also building database-driven applications that organize, analyze, and transform information into something useful. I also enjoy developing complete applications from the ground up, including designing databases, creating user interfaces, connecting backend systems, and bringing ideas into working software. Whether it is a game analytics tool, a mobile application, or an embedded Raspberry Pi project, I enjoy learning new technologies and using them to turn ideas into reality.
