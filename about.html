About Me

Hello! My name is D'vin Johnson. I am a software developer who enjoys building projects that combine creativity, problem-solving, and technology. My main interests are Artificial Intelligence, Database Systems, and Software Development. I enjoy creating applications ranging from AI systems to database-driven tools inspired by the technology and games I enjoy.

My Story

My interest in software and technology began with my father, who has worked with these technologies throughout my life. Watching him work became one of my biggest influences in pursuing a career focused on software development and database systems.

Another major influence has been my passion for video games. I have had a controller in my hand for as long as I can remember, and my love for gaming eventually grew into curiosity about the technology behind the experiences I enjoyed.

Learning how my favorite games handled systems, mechanics, and data sparked my interest in understanding how software works behind the scenes.

This curiosity led me to pursue my Bachelor of Science in Computer and Information Sciences at Harrisburg University of Science and Technology. During my studies, I developed experience with multiple programming languages, software development practices, database systems, and artificial intelligence.

Through experiences such as my internship with the Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, my contract position at Foot Locker as a Server Technician, and my current role as an IT Help Desk Technician at Lincoln Intermediate Unit 12, I have continued developing my technical skills while discovering the direction I want to take in my professional career.

What I Build

Artificial Intelligence

I enjoy exploring artificial intelligence through projects involving computer vision, machine learning, and intelligent systems. My AI projects focus on creating applications that can process information, recognize patterns, and interact with the world around them.

Database Systems

I enjoy designing database systems that organize information and transform data into something useful. Many of my projects involve creating database structures, connecting applications to stored data, and developing systems that analyze and display meaningful information.

Software Development

I enjoy building complete applications from the ground up, including designing databases, creating user interfaces, connecting backend systems, and turning ideas into working software. Whether it is a game analytics tool, mobile application, or embedded Raspberry Pi project, I enjoy learning new technologies and creating solutions.

Outside of Programming

Outside of programming, there are two main places you can usually find me: at the theater watching the newest horror movie or at my PC exploring the many games I enjoy.

Currently, some of my favorite games include Dead by Daylight, Smite 2, Apex Legends, and RimWorld. Gaming has always been a major influence in my interest in technology and continues to inspire many of my personal projects.

I also enjoy tabletop games such as Dungeons & Dragons with my friends, even if my dice rolls do not always cooperate.
