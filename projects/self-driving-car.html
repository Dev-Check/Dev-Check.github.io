Self-Driving Car AI

A two-semester artificial intelligence project focused on creating a self-driving vehicle capable of learning how to navigate using machine learning techniques, computer vision concepts, and autonomous decision making.

Project Overview

This project was developed over two semesters as both a research and software engineering project. My goal was to understand how artificial intelligence can make driving decisions using sensor data rather than hard-coded movement.

Throughout development I researched autonomous driving techniques, designed the project architecture, implemented the application in Python, and evaluated how different AI behaviors affected driving performance.

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges during development was creating AI behavior that could consistently navigate the environment without relying on hard-coded movement.

The project required experimenting with different approaches, debugging unexpected behaviors, and refining how the AI interpreted information before making driving decisions.

What I Learned

This project significantly expanded my understanding of artificial intelligence development, research methodology, software architecture, and large-scale Python applications.

It also strengthened my ability to break large problems into manageable components while documenting research, testing solutions, and improving software through iterative development.
